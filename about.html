The story behind TARMIM PRO SERVICES BROKER - L.L.C. and what we're building for UAE households.

Why TARMIM exists

The problem

Finding a reliable maintenance vendor in the UAE usually means asking around, calling a few numbers, and hoping the price is fair.

The fix

One app where the request goes out once, vendors compete honestly, and the whole job is tracked on a single ticket.

TARMIM PRO SERVICES BROKER - L.L.C. is building an app that acts as a broker between UAE households and home maintenance vendors. Instead of calling around for a plumber, painter, electrician, or handyman and hoping for a fair price, residents describe their problem once inside the app.

Vendors on the platform review the request, respond with their price and what the job involves, and the resident chooses who to go with. Once a vendor is selected, the work is carried out and tracked through to completion -- with everyone working from the same ticket.

No more asking three neighbours for a number that turns out to be disconnected. One request, several honest quotes, one job done properly.
