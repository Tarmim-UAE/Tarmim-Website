How it works

Three steps. No middle-man calls.

The whole flow happens on one ticket, from the first description to the final job sign-off.

01 Describe the problem

Log the issue

Tell TARMIM what's wrong -- a leaking tap, a wall that needs paint, an AC that's given up. Add photos, your emirate, and how urgent it is.

02 Vendors respond

Compare real quotes

Vetted vendors review the ticket and reply with their price and what the job involves. You pick who to go with -- no guesswork, no cold calls.

03 Work gets done

Job carried out

The chosen vendor completes the work and the ticket is closed in-app, so there's a record of what was agreed and what was delivered.
